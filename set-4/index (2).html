How to play: The 16 sliders above are your wearable's neural signal (alpha/beta/gamma/theta/delta EEG, heart rate, motion, GSR, etc.). Move them to change your "thought." The 3 nearest cells in the Quilt light up below. The cosine number shows how close you are — closer = warmer. In DUET mode, two wearers play Marco Polo: align your thoughts for higher warmth. In JAM mode, many wearers' signals average into one hive-mind query.
