## Which JEPA, precisely

The dissertation's use of JEPA follows the LeCun (2022) line — **predict-latent, not generative, not contrastive**: the model predicts representations in embedding space, never pixels/tokens, and never via contrastive negatives. The elephant's implementation recipe is the standard one: EMA target encoder + stop-gradient + variance regularization (VICReg-style) over dial time-series (v1), with v3's room-state embeddings trained on cold/warm contrast. **Two doctrinal choices distinguish this fleet's use from the stock recipe:**

1. **The unit of perception is the room (the ensemble field), not the stream (the message sequence).** Latents are predicted over windowed room-states — the vMF (μ̂, κ) snapshot of the DialBank's 7-dial readings — not over token/clip sequences. Justification: the empirical probe chain (encoder within-room fine gap 0.015 vs cross-room 0.271-doc-claim; dial-tier within-night 1.229) plus the doctrine's contrast-only training rule.
2. **JEPA is the *sense*, not the model.** One JEPA is a dial; the elephant is the DialBank ensemble; readings *nudge* (blend ≤ 0.15) and never replace. The contrast head (2026-08-19) is the one place the fleet crossed into contrastive learning proper (InfoNCE, multi-positive, room positives) — and it is labeled as such in Ch5, not smuggled under the JEPA name. ## The outside-the-box challenge, answered honestly (hermes pass)

**"Maybe there's no invariant at all" — JEPA and polyformalism as co-conspirators avoiding an empty overlap.** Does the discipline cover it? Partly, and the part it doesn't cover is real. Covered: the hermes worst case ("perfect replicability, every threshold hit, measuring nothing") is not a hypothetical — the apparatus already caught one instance of it (the 0.478 in-sample "PASS" that collapsed to held-out 0.0694; the phantom 0.271 baseline that existed only in docs). The discipline's answer to "you measured nothing" is the held-out room, the null control, the treatment sensitivity — and those are exactly the instruments that fired. Not covered: a *global* emptiness claim — that room-temperature itself has no invariant, that the dial tier is "measuring the clock, not the room" (idea 2) — cannot be excluded by within-frame tests, because every within-frame test presupposes the frame. The honest position: **the dissertation's claims are all conditional on the frame having an object, and the frame-level falsifier is registered as the silence test** (hermes idea 1, adopted as a test, not an objection): rerun the classification suite with inter-event silence durations as the *only* features; if scores match without any edge content, the apparatus has been measuring the clock, and the dissertation says so. **Registration constraint (advisor flag, pre-run): the silence feature's SOURCE must be specified, and STEP=60 auto-clock sessions are EXCLUDED from the test corpus** — synthetic strictly-increasing timestamps make silence durations constant there, so the test would trivially pass or fail without discriminating anything. The test only has teeth where the clock varies: real-timed sessions (tap/radio-theater transcripts with genuine pacing) or a text-length-derived reading-time proxy. Silence-as-data (the hesitation before the step) is also booked as a logged-but-unread feature of the edge log — the cheapest honest upgrade available. Idea 3 itself (no invariant) is neither adopted nor rejected: it is the standing alternative hypothesis, and the silence test is what keeps it honest.
