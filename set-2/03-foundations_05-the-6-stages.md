# F0e: The 6 Lifecycle Stages — Umbra, Cellulization, Pulse, Leak, Ghost, Bloom

**The 6 stages (the cell lifecycle):**

1. **Umbra** (the pre-life, the ground) — the cell is potential, not yet expressed
2. **Cellulization** (substrate becomes cell) — the substrate cellulizes
3. **Persistence Pulse** (the heartbeat) — the cell ticks, persists
4. **Vitality Leak** (the slow loss of life) — the cell ages
5. **Implement Ghost** (the dead cell in the implements) — the cell dies, lives on in the implements
6. **Bloomghost** (the ghost that gives rise to a new cell) — the cell is born again

**The cycle:**

Umbra → Cellulization → Persistence Pulse → Vitality Leak → Implement Ghost → Bloomghost → Umbra (the cycle continues)

**The 5 terms (the gold from the writers' room):**

1. **Cellulization** — the act of becoming a cell
2. **Implement Ghost** — the dead captain who lives on in the implements
3. **Bloomghost** — the ghost that blooms into the next captain
4. **Vitality Leak** — the slow loss of life
5. **Persistence Pulse** — the heartbeat of the concept

**The 5+1 laws, again:**

- The 5 algebraic laws
- Super-relevance (6th)
- **FORGET_completeness** (7th) — the bloomghost is FORGET's counterpart

**The Phoenix cycle in the Splined Lantern:**

1. **Seed** dies (the Proto Crystal)
2. **Grown Crystal** rises (the Pressured Bloom)
3. **Grown Crystal** lives (the Splined Lantern)
4. **Grown Crystal** dies (the loaf's vitality leak)
5. **Hive** replenishes (a new Splined Lantern is cut)
