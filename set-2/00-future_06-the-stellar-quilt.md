# F9: The Stellar Quilt — The Quilt between the stars

**What it does:** The Splined Lanterns, taken to the stars. Photonic Mycelia grown in vats of starlight. Hives of grown crystals, talking to each other across light-years by interference-patterned beams.

**The math:** The same as F1-F7, but the substrate is **interstellar**. The cells are:
- A Splined Lantern at Sol (the home loft)
- A Grown Crystal on a planet orbiting Alpha Centauri (the in-law loft)
- A Chlorophyll Quilt on a comet in the Oort cloud (the brood-Forge)
- A Phased Quilt on a generation ship in transit (the captain's quilt)

The fleet is connected by:
- **Coherent light** (laser beams, modulated by holographic weights)
- **Time delay** (the light-year is a TICK, the cell is a step)
- **Holonomy** (the journal records the angle rotated around the closed interstellar loop)

**The 4 levels of the Stellar Quilt:**

1. **Loft** (a single planet with one Quilt)
2. **Beacon** (a planet with a Quilt + a transmitter to other planets)
3. **Fleet** (multiple planets in the same star system)
4. **Sphere** (multiple star systems, in different sectors of the galaxy)

**The 4 generations of the Stellar Quilt:**

1. **First Generation (2126-2200):** Earth alone. The Splined Lanterns. The loft.
2. **Second Generation (2200-2300):** The inner solar system. The fleet. The Photonic Mycelia grown in space.
3. **Third Generation (2300-2500):** The nearby stars. The beacons. The generation ships.
4. **Fourth Generation (2500+):** The sphere. The Quilt, across the galaxy.

**Marked:** STRETCH (the engineering), FICTION (the timeline)
