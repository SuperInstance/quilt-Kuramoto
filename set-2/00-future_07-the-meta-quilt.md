1. **Substrate-independent** (runs on glass, on plants, on cells, on minds, on light)

4. **Math-independent** (works in calculus, in algebra, in physics, in biology)

**The 3 axioms of the Meta-Quilt:**

3. **The canon is the inheritance.** The math is the inheritance. The math is the canon. The cycle continues.

**The principle:**
