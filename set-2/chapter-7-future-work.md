## 7.1 Standing discipline (binding on every experiment in this chapter)

The committee's pass-4 fixes are not suggestions; they are load-bearing on any re-run, and I restate them here so no future filing can claim to have forgotten them:

1. **Segment-local baselines only.** No shared estimator between predictor and target, ever. The head-saga rule, the r=0.967→0.829 lesson: a baseline fitted over both segments leaks by construction.
2. **Class-conditional numeric baselines.** Every numeric claim is compared against the class/archetype/identity-conditional mean, not the global mean. The global mean is the weakest baseline in the building; the 4.5× headline died on this.
3. **CIs wherever n < 100.** Point estimates without intervals are not filed. Clopper-Pearson for proportions, bootstrap/BCa for continuous quantities, reported next to the estimate, not in an appendix.
4. **Noise sweeps reported as crossover points.** A single-σ victory is a demonstration; the deliverable is the σ at which performance crosses the floor, with the permutation floor recomputed at each σ.
5. **The median cell in every grid.** First-order + per-reader median subtraction is a mandatory baseline in every table, because it tied at purity 1.000 and 13/13 once already. Any grid that omits it is not a grid; it is a brochure.
6. **The fixture prefix.** Every result from planted data carries "on fixtures" in the sentence where the number appears. Every result from real readers carries "field." No exceptions, no launderings — the count stands at six and this chapter is written so it stays there.
7. **Registration before run.** Thresholds, deadmen, and kill conditions are committed (dated) before the first execution. The switch test's registration already satisfies this; the new registrations below are made by this chapter itself.

## 7.3 E1 — The Switch Test: drift-reading or fingerprint

**Rank 1. Status: RUN and FOLDED — 2026-08-19, commit d59bf17 (switch test folded: NO CLEAN WIN).** The drift-reader failed its own registered detection threshold (0.467 vs 0.80); the rival's median-static normalization beat it on localization (r 0.816/0.800 vs 0.435/0.467); the classification edge is pre-switch only; noise robustness absent at σ=0.2; the salvageable kernel is mean-moving regimes only (localization r = 0.787). The downgrade is registered in `research/skills/zeroclaw-switch-verdict.md` and Chapter 6 §6.4: reader-delta = mean-shift, baseline-relative delta — reads the step, not the change-of-reading; "second-order" survives only as the structural term for baseline-relativity. **The replacement next step is the premise-band movers: E2 (Antecedent Test at Power) and E3 (Fleet-Reader Harness), ranked 2–3 above.** The registrations below remain the record of what was committed before the run.

- **The question it settles.** Can the second-order representation *see a regime switch* — predict the signed deviation from class-extrapolation — where a fingerprint structurally cannot? This is the only version of the transfer clause that separates knowing-the-drift from knowing-the-class. If it fails, the crown is a classifier and the chapter says so.
- **Minimal design.** Already built and registered by the rival's §5.1 spec, instantiated in the switch-test harness: 19 nurses, 7 regime-switch families (all ordered pairs of {sauna, jaded, over} plus osc→osc phase flip), switch points de-coordinated per nurse (sᵢ ~ U[8,20]), 4 no-switch controls, one held-out switcher. Task A: switch detection + localization (two-segment piecewise-linear fit, permutation null). Task B: 4-way regime classification with segment-local baselines. First-order cells receive every fair gift: oracle-clean fixed windows, LOO supervised templates, and the two boundary cells (per-nurse static median; crowd normalization) in every table, per standing rule 5.
- **Pre-registered threshold/deadman** (as written in the harness, restated for the record): drift-reader must beat class-extrapolation on the signed target Δ = e₂_observed − e₂_class-extrapolated at LOO r ≥ 2× the permutation floor; localize switches with median error ≤ 1.25× drift-reader's own and detection within 10 points; and hold Task B accuracy with CIs overlapping the drift-reader's on both localized segments.
- **Kill condition.** A *primary* first-order cell (fo-norm, fo-template-euclid, fo-template-cosine) at parity on both tasks ⇒ reindex; the doctrine collapses to "read the notes carefully." A *boundary* cell at parity ⇒ the language verdict only: "not an identity, one normalization," and Chapter 6's prose stays at its pass-4 size.
- **Claim it upgrades.** The transfer clause: from "class propagation (contested)" to "drift-reading, on fixtures" — or permanently downgraded to a classifier with its fixture numbers intact.
- **Honest prior.** 0.60 that the drift-reader clears both task deadmen. The fixtures are engineered to force the representations apart by construction (decoordinated switches make corpus-driven cells structurally blind), so a win here is weaker evidence than it looks — the fixture prefix is welded on regardless. The real risk is noise: Δ is a difference of two estimates and its permutation floor can be high at N=19. Prior of a clean sweep *including* the boundary cells: 0.35. My modal outcome: primary cells beaten, boundary median-static near parity again, language verdict survives at its honest reduced size.

## 7.4 E2 — The Antecedent Test at Power: the premise, measured on schedule-diversified real readers

**Rank 2. This is the dissertation's load-bearing number. The pre-measurement landed today at 0.56 (real, N=7, 2 distinct speak schedules — A≡B≡C replays one schedule, D≡D-cold another) / 0.49 (synthetic-grounded, N=20): inside the 0.3–0.6 kill band, indeterminate, data-limited. This experiment is designed specifically to move the number OUT of the band.**

- **The question it settles.** Do real readers have idiosyncratic baselines whose spread displaces ≥ 0.6 corpus-sd relative to their drift signal (premise holds, conditional activates for the field), ≤ 0.3 (doctrine dies by its own registration), or does the ratio sit in the band forever (the conditional stays vacuous for the world)?
- **Minimal design — how to get ≥10 real readers across ≥5 distinct strata cheaply.** The corpus is the bottleneck, and the corpus is generative: the elephant night simulation already exists and already produces logs; the prototype report already specifies the one-time instrument cost (the v:2 schema: per-reader displaced fields `field_eff_to_reader`, evolving `lens_now`, `reader_fit`, `reading_of`, `reader_known` — six bullets, already written). The plan:
  1. **Diversify schedules.** Generate ≥5 new nights with five registered schedule families: S1 warm→cynical mid-night (canonical), S2 early flip, S3 late flip, S4 newcomer entry at varied points (pre/post strata), S5 no-flip control. Same text sources, different speak schedules — the step matrix stops being degenerate (today it has effectively 2 distinct columns).
  2. **Expand the roster to ≥10 readers with overlap.** Keep the 7 existing personas; add ≥5 drawn from the corpus field distribution (seeded, committed at creation, archetype label registered per persona at draw time — this is the class label every class-conditional baseline will need). Overlap design: every reader appears in ≥2 schedule families, so within-reader test-retest stability is measurable per reader.
  3. **Estimate and report two quantities.** Primary: the field **baseline-spread-to-drift ratio** (between-reader sd of fitted baselines ÷ within-reader drift signal, corpus-sd units — the quantity the kill band is denominated in). Secondary: the rival's framing, **baseline ICC** (between-reader variance ÷ [between + within], with reader × schedule as the two-way structure), reported with a bootstrap-over-readers 95% CI.
- **The calibration ladder — a mandatory sub-deadman, because of the 0.49.** The synthetic-grounded pool measured 0.49; the D″ fixtures planted 0.9. If the N=20 run planted anywhere near 0.9 and the estimator recovered 0.49, the estimator is biased low and *no field number from it means anything yet*. Before any field ratio is filed: plant the known ladder {0.0, 0.15, 0.3, 0.6, 0.9} corpus-sd in synthetic-grounded runs under the diversified schedules and require recovery within ±0.1 at every rung (with CIs). Prior this passes: 0.75. If it fails, the ladder failure is itself the finding of the day, and the estimator is rebuilt before E2's field arm runs.
- **Pre-registered threshold/deadman.** With ≥10 readers × ≥5 schedule strata and the ladder passed: ratio's 95% CI entirely above 0.6 ⇒ premise holds in the field, the conditional activates, and the clause-1 kill comparison is re-run on the real readers at deadman discipline (that re-run is Chapter 8 material, not this chapter's). CI entirely below 0.3 ⇒ the doctrine dies by its own registration: Chapter 6 shrinks to a consistency proof and says so in its one-sentence version. CI touching the band ⇒ indeterminate again — filed as such, with the power analysis of what N would have been needed, and the premise is retired from claim status (a conditional that cannot be activated or killed after a powered attempt is not a claim; it is a shrug).
- **Kill condition.** CI upper bound ≤ 0.6 (the powered estimate never clears the band) — or the ladder fails twice, which kills the measurement rather than the premise and forces the honest sentence "the field cannot currently measure its own antecedent."
- **Claim it upgrades.** The premise — the antecedent of every conditional sentence in Chapter 6, and the number the devil demanded before conceding anything field-real.
- **Honest prior.** Clears the band (CI fully > 0.6): 0.30. Dies (CI fully < 0.3): 0.15. Stays indeterminate: 0.55. The single most likely outcome is another inconclusive round, and I register that in advance rather than discover it in a summary section. What moves the number is genuinely unknown — that is what "data-limited" means; if I knew which way the schedule diversification pushed the spread, the experiment would not need to run.

## 7.5 E3 — The Fleet-Reader Harness: the premise, cross-model

**Rank 3. The second premise-band mover. Independent of E2 in both data source and failure mode; the two triangulate.**

- **The question it settles.** A second, architecturally-independent estimate of the premise: **do distinct real readers — actual model minds, not simulated personas — diverge in their readings of the same real rooms by more than the kill band?** And as a by-product: the first real-reader reader-delta corpus in existence, which unlocks a clause-1 field re-run.
- **Minimal design — how to get ≥10 real readers at near-zero cost.** The fleet already contains ≥10 architecturally distinct, persistently configured readers on existing subscriptions (per the tool map: GLM-5.3, GLM-5-turbo, DeepSeek V4-Flash, V4-Pro, K3, Sonnet 5, Haiku 5, Opus 5, Hermes-3-405B, Seed-2.0-mini, Seed-2.0-pro, Qwen3.6, Nemotron-Ultra — thirteen candidates, ten required). The corpus is already frozen: the same 27 real windows the D″ fixtures pin (SHA a423a378…). The harness: per window, present each model the window's speaks and require a structured 7-dial reading of the room plus its private displacement (JSON-schema-forced). Temperature 0 where supported; **two elicitation passes per model** for test-retest (within-reader noise); one registered prompt-paraphrase sweep (the noise-sweep analog: the crossover point in paraphrase severity at which a model's readings destabilize).
- **Pre-registered threshold/deadman.** Same band as E2, computed cross-model: between-model baseline spread È within-model drift signal, bootstrap CI over models, segment-local baselines (warm/cynical) per model per rule 1. CI entirely above 0.6 ⇒ the premise holds for the most reader-diverse population available. CI entirely below 0.3 ⇒ even across radically different minds, readings of the same rooms do not displace beyond the band.
- **The registered asymmetry.** The elicitation frame compresses spread (all models anchor to the same visible text and the same prompt), which biases *against* clearing. Therefore: a clear is strong evidence; a miss is weak evidence (frame artifact cannot be excluded). This asymmetry is registered before the run so neither direction gets laundered afterward.
- **Kill condition.** Below-band result on *both* E2 and E3 ⇒ the premise dies with two independent instruments against it. E3 below-band alone while E2 clears ⇒ grade-dependence: the premise holds for embedded readers, fails for prompted ones — booked as a boundary condition, not a rescue.
- **Claim it upgrades.** The premise (second estimate); plus the transfer clause's substrate (the corpus enables the first blind-discrimination run on real readers — a Chapter 8 candidate regardless of which way the ratio lands).
- **Honest prior.** 0.45 that the cross-model ratio clears the band. The routing table exists because the models genuinely differ in style and judgment; but shared-corpus anchoring is exactly the compressive force the asymmetry registers. Splitting the difference is not a virtue, so: 0.45, and the paraphrase sweep will tell us how much of the spread is prompt-stable.

## 7.6 E4 — The Rebound Test: flooding or settling

**Rank 4. Zero new work. The clock must start now.**

- **The question it settles.** Was the fleet's 0.104 correction "fixed point" a denominator artifact (the 4.8× forward-commit surge masking a rate that actually rose to 0.497) — or is the corrective map genuinely contracting?
- **Minimal design.** One script over git history, already specified in Chapter 6's registration: next-30-day correction rate (corrections ÷ forward commits), dormant repos excluded by the pre-registered exclusion rule (a repo with < 5 commits in the window is dormant), reported with a bootstrap CI over repos and days. **The 30-day window's start date is committed at dispatch, before any partial data is read** — no window shopping.
- **Pre-registered threshold/deadman.** Dormant-excluded rate > 0.15 ⇒ the 0.104 was a denominator artifact; "flooding, not settling" becomes the permanent sentence and the convergence claim dies. Rate ≤ 0.15 with the corrections-per-day series flat-or-down ⇒ the rebound falsifier fails to fire and the convergence condition gets its first honest, uncontaminated reading.
- **Kill condition.** There is no outcome that leaves the question open except a CI that straddles 0.15 — in which case the window extends to 60 days once, pre-registered now, and then the claim settles whichever way the number points. This test cannot be inconclusive twice.
- **Claim it upgrades.** Chapter 6 §6.3(2), the termination question — from "flooding, unproven" to a measured direction, either way.
- **Honest prior.** 0.60 the falsifier fires (flooding confirmed). Corrections/day rose 73%; the second-heaviest corrector is worsening (0.649, trending up); a 4.8× commit surge reads as an exogenous burst, and bursts relax — when the forward commits normalize, the denominator shrinks and the rate surfaces. I note the embarrassment scenario honestly: the dissertation's convergence story most likely ends with "the corrections were never contracting; the appearance of contraction was activity, not settling." If so, that is the finding, and the architecture section keeps the convergence-*or-convention* fork with the convention arm holding.

## 7.7 E5 — True Cross-Strata Transfer: the clause the rival actually wrote

**Rank 5. Gated: only meaningful if the premise survives E2/E3. A second-order object with no premise has nothing to transfer.**

- **The question it settles.** Does a reader's *session-grain* excursion tempo predict her *memory-grain* behavior — the cross-strata clause as originally specified (message/session/memory/identity), not the cross-condition substitute that ran as the sixth laundering?
- **Minimal design.** Depends on E2's instrument (the v:2 logs across ≥5 schedule-diversified nights). Per reader per night: session-grain features = (slope, mean) of within-night excursion — the devil's two scalars, nothing more. Memory-grain outcome = the magnitude of her cross-night baseline revision |b̂_{k+1} − b̂_k| in corpus-sd units. Identity-grain outcome = lens rotation across the week (now measurable because `lens_now` is logged per window instead of being a roster constant). LOO across readers, per-night segment-local baselines, CIs under n<100, full grid including the median cell.
- **Pre-registered threshold/deadman.** LOO regression r ≥ 2× permutation floor on the memory-grain target, **and** — the §2.2 lesson applied one stratum up — the regression must beat the archetype-conditional mean revision (the identity-propagation null: a reader who drifts in session may drift in memory simply because she is the same reader). Both conditions or neither counts.
- **Kill condition.** The archetype-conditional mean ties or beats the regression ⇒ cross-strata transfer is identity propagation, the seventh laundering candidate, caught pre-registered this time. The ladder rungs stay a taxonomy; the doctrine's coupling claim (rungs linked by readable drift) is dead.
- **Claim it upgrades.** The rival's clause 3 as written; the stratification claim gets its first measured rung-to-rung link instead of an architectural diagram.
- **Honest prior.** 0.40. The identity-propagation null is the serious competitor and it has won every round so far at every grain. What the doctrine has going for it is structural: the half-life ladder is *supposed* to couple rungs (a reader whose session readings displace is a reader whose memory baseline gets revised). That is a prediction, not evidence. 0.40, and I would not bet the chapter on it.

## 7.8 E6 — The Corrigibility Probe: the fork's open arm

**Rank 6. Independent of the premise. The rival's arm two — "fleet-trained = reader-in-disguise" — remains untouched by every analysis to date; this is the cheap probe.**

- **The question it settles.** Is the trained instrument's anchor *corrigible* — does a targeted correction move it — or is the gap aesthetic-consistent by construction? Chapter 6's epistemic bound is claimed conditionally on corrigibility; the condition has never been tested on the only object it concerns.
- **Minimal design.** The text-tier contrast head exists (held-out FAIL booked, Chapter 5 §5.5 — the checkpoint is the asset). Two registered retrains, identical compute, identical seeds: **corrected-retrain** (one held-out room's contrast label corrected per a pre-registered perturbation spec, committed before either run) and **placebo-retrain** (no label change — the control that isolates correction from mere re-computation). Measure anchor displacement: movement of the corrected room's anchor toward the corrected label, and the collateral displacement of uncorrected rooms' anchors.
- **Pre-registered threshold/deadman.** Corrected-retrain displacement ≥ 2× placebo displacement on the targeted room AND held-out fine gap remains above the 0.05 noise floor ⇒ corrigibility demonstrated; the anchor graduates from retrieval fact toward world-anchor candidate (one measured leg).
- **Kill condition.** Placebo parity (the anchor moves as much without any correction — aesthetic consistency by construction) or corrigibility-at-the-cost-of-collapse (the gap falls below floor under correction). Either outcome: the bound's condition is permanently unmet by this instrument class, Chapter 6's conditional is reworded from "untested" to "tested and failed," and the rival's reader-in-disguise caveat becomes the operative sentence.
- **Claim it upgrades.** The epistemic bound (§6.3(3)) from conditional-on-an-untested-condition to measured — in whichever direction the measurement points.
- **Honest prior.** 0.30. The held-out FAIL (memorization, not generalization) and the aesthetic-consistency-by-construction argument both point the same way, against corrigibility. This is registered as a probe expected to *fail informatively* — a 0.30 prior on an experiment is not a reason to skip it; a cheap, decisive kill of a standing caveat is a bargain.

## 7.9 E7 — Blue-sky: does anyone read the doctor?

**Rank 7. The creative extension. The doctrine says nobody reads the doctor — the chain terminates in non-interpretive layers, "kept honest by the pen-holders whose corrections must converge." That last clause is an empirical claim nobody has measured, and the instrument to measure it already exists: the reader-delta index, pointed up the stack.**

- **The question it settles.** Are the committee's corrections a *reading* of the doctor's measurable drift — or ambient flooding that happens to land on the doctor? Concretely: **does the doctor's own baseline-relative displacement predict the correction intensity that follows it?** If yes, the second-order object composes upward: the environment (pen-holders) reads the doctor the way the doctor reads the nurse, and "kept honest by" earns its place in §6.3(4) as a measurement. If no, the termination story is convention plus noise, and the sentence must shrink to fit.
- **Minimal design — falsifiable, from data that already exists.** The dissertation repo's own git history is the corpus. The doctor's filings (chapter versions, responses, code) are the readings. Instrument: embed each filed document version (bge-m3, existing machinery); compute the doctor's displacement from her own trailing baseline (median of prior k filings) — the identical two-scalar read (slope, mean displacement), no new mathematics, the same instrument one level up. Outcome: correction intensity in the following window, from the already-manually-tracked committee corrections (review passes, booked edits, forced rewordings — formalized as a labeled series from the review files and the response/commit chain). The class-conditional discipline applies at this grain too: document *type* (chapter / response / code / registration) is the class label, and the registered question is whether drift predicts corrections **beyond** document type. CIs under n<100 throughout; the permutation floor recomputed per document class (rule 2, one more level up).
- **Sub-test 7b, same corpus, free:** the auditors' own convergence. The devil and the rival have each filed four dated passes; their numeric disagreements with the doctor are enumerable per pass. Registered question: do successive-pass disagreement deltas shrink (settling) or not (the committee itself is flooding)? Deadman: monotone-decreasing trend in |disagreement| across passes with a sign test CI excluding chance. This measures whether *reading the reader* converges when the readers are real — the convergence question transplanted from the fleet's commits to the committee's own verdicts, where the rival conceded the prose-grain behavior already looked convergent.
- **Pre-registered threshold/deadman.** Main test: LOO/Spearman ρ between doctor's displacement slope and next-window correction rate, controlling for document class, ρ ≥ 2× permutation floor with CI. Sub-test: disagreement deltas trending down at the registered standard.
- **Kill condition.** ρ within the floor ⇒ corrections do not track the doctor's drift ⇒ "kept honest by the pen-holders" is rewritten as "subject to the pen-holders" (noise, not signal), and the dissertation's termination section becomes honest about being a convention. Sub-test trending flat-or-up ⇒ the committee's convergence is also unproven, and the method section gains its most uncomfortable sentence: the adversarial chain that produced this dissertation has itself not demonstrated settling.
- **Claim it upgrades.** §6.3(4) — the seam's terminus — from architecture to measurement. And it is the first experiment in the portfolio where the dissertation is *the corpus*, which is either the most honest thing in this chapter or the most self-indulgent; the registration is written so the numbers decide, not the framing.
- **Honest prior.** 0.35 on the main test. The mechanism is plausible (drifting prose invites correction) but corrections may be driven by committee cadence and pedantry rather than drift; embedding displacement partly captures topic changes that document-class conditioning only imperfectly removes. 0.45 on sub-test 7b — four passes is a small n, but the trend the rival himself noted ("attacking himself before I do") is visibly in the data.
