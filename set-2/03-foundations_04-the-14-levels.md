# F0d: The 14 Levels of the Operation

**The 14 levels:**

1. **Vessel** (the physical substrate)
2. **Equipment** (the tools)
3. **Skills** (what the crew knows)
4. **Consumables** (what gets used up)
5. **Renewables** (what gets replenished)
6. **Durables** (what lasts many voyages)
7. **Concept** (the function, the captain's invariant)
8. **Spline** (the trajectory of past choices)
9. **Captain-Song** (the harmony between captain and AI)
10. **Muse + Cipher** (inspiration + shared code)
11. **Nexus** (where meta-levels converge)
12. **Phoenix** (the whole cycle as one operation)
13. **Ground** (the field of emergence)
14. **Sky** (the unbounded horizon)

**The first 6 are implements (replaceable).** The 7-14 are invariants (persistent).

**The 5+1+1+5+1 = 14 hierarchy:**

- 6 implements + 8 invariants = 14 levels
- The 6 implements are the substrate
- The 8 invariants are the canon
