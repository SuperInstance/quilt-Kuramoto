## fiction-kaldfjord

A sustained historical pastiche: the invented "Kaldfjord Circle", a 1903-05
Norwegian fishing-village learned society whose members reinvent consensus,
double-entry bookkeeping and staleness/latency tradeoffs in period voice. It
names its own generating models per character — the editor's preface credits
GLM-5.3, DeepSeek V4-Pro, Hermes-405B and others. The historical frame disciplines
the prose in a way the rest of the corpus is not, which makes it the most
readable writing in the archive.

## Honest read

Most of this is one metaphor (the five opcodes) re-skinned against arbitrary
nouns — the river, the kitchen, the weather, the city. Two-thirds of the papers
have no text at all. The load-bearing technical content is in
`code/quilt-verilog/docs/`, not here.
