Negative Space — leaks in the double-entry ledger

How to play: Adjust the sliders for the game state and your body readings. The "Your model" is what you say about yourself. The "Your body" is the wearable. The "Game stream" is the ground truth. The integrity score is the audit of all three. The leaks below are where the model, body, and game disagree.

Try this experiment:
  1. Set body eeg_beta=80, eeg_alpha=20, HR=70, GSR=80. You're physiologically stressed.
  2. Set model focus=80, calm=80. You're claiming focus and calm.
  3. Watch the integrity crash to ~0.65 and the leaks explode.
  4. Now move model focus=40, calm=30. The leaks shrink; integrity rises.
The truth is, the model can lie. The body can't. The game can't.
