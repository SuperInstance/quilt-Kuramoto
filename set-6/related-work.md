## A. The drift-instrument family (the reader-delta's ancestors)

The committee's hardest sentence — the devil's — was: *"The reader-delta index is CUSUM pointed at a nurse."* Entries A1–A4 are that sentence, unpacked. Read together, they say: **the instrument is a century old; the fleet did not build a new instrument.**

### A1. Shewhart control charts (statistical process control), with CUSUM

- **What it is.** The foundational instrument of industrial statistics/quality control: a process is judged not by its output's absolute level but by its **displacement from the process's own in-control baseline**, with decision thresholds (control limits) derived from the process's own measured variability. Walter A. Shewhart, Bell Telephone Laboratories; the first control chart appears in his 16 May 1924 Bell Labs memorandum (primary source is an internal memo — flagged below), with the canonical book being *Economic Control of Quality of Manufactured Product* (Van Nostrand, 1931). The cumulative-sum extension — accumulating small persistent displacements so slow drifts become detectable — is E. S. Page, "Continuous Inspection Schemes," *Biometrika* 41(1/2):100–115, 1954.
- **What the dissertation borrows.** The entire instrument shape of the reader-delta: a per-unit fitted baseline (the nurse's median), displacement from it *as the signal* (‖r − b̂‖), thresholds set relative to the unit's own noise floor (the ≥2× floor discipline, the deadman's idiom), and the two-scalar read the devil's ablations isolated (slope and mean of the displacement trajectory) — a trend/CUSUM-style summary of accumulated deviation. The premise (an idiosyncratic in-control state worth monitoring) is Shewhart's premise.
- **What is genuinely new.** Only the **placement**: the "process" under control is *another reader's readings of a third object* — a committee member's outputs about a room, not a widget, a sensor, or a production model — and the displacement is used as a **retrieval key into a stratified memory architecture** (the half-life ladder), not as an accept/reject alarm. That is an application of a classical instrument, and it is **fixture-conditional**: proven on planted fixtures only; the field instrument (per-reader logs) does not exist yet; the kill band is 0.3–0.6 corpus-sd; and the margin over first-order-plus-normalization is, per Rival A's median-trick run, definitional rather than empirical. Say all of that wherever this entry is cited.

### A2. Jacobson & Truax — the Reliable Change Index (RCI)

- **What it is.** Clinical psychology's standard instrument for judging whether an *individual's* change is real: displacement from the individual's own baseline, judged against measurement error. N. S. Jacobson & P. Truax, "Clinical Significance: A Statistical Approach to Defining Change in Psychotherapy," *Journal of Consulting and Clinical Psychology* 59(1):12–19, 1991. The RCI proper is (x₂ − x₁)/SE; the field-accepted idea is "reliable change" as a baseline-relative, error-normalized step.
- **What the dissertation borrows.** The exact doctrine sentence — "the felt size of the step *from her own baseline*" — is reliable change, minus the word "felt." The devil said it precisely: the honest minimal reader-delta is *"subtract her baseline, take the magnitude, keep the slope and the mean"* — Jacobson–Truax's two-line recipe with a norm collapse. Chapter 6 §6.2 concedes this by name.
- **What is genuinely new.** Nothing in the statistics; the same placement residual as A1 (the baseline belongs to a *reader of a room*, and the excursion is consumed as a retrieval key). This entry exists mostly to keep the dissertation from claiming A1's residual twice.

### A3. GMM-UBM speaker verification

- **What it is.** The canonical 1990s architecture of text-independent speaker verification: fit a **Universal Background Model** over a large population, adapt it (MAP) to each enrolled speaker, and verify a new utterance by its displacement (likelihood ratio) from the *enrolled model* relative to the background — judging the sample against a model of the known individual rather than by the sample's content; evaluated in calibrated detection metrics (d′/EER; DET curves — see C1). Origin: D. A. Reynolds & R. C. Rose, "Robust text-independent speaker identification using Gaussian mixture speaker models," *IEEE Transactions on Speech and Audio Processing* 3(1), 1995; canonical statement: D. A. Reynolds, T. F. Quatieri, R. B. Dunn, "Speaker verification using adapted Gaussian mixture models," *Digital Signal Processing* 10(1–3):19–41, 2000.
- **What the dissertation borrows.** The **structure, whole**: enroll the known reader (fit her baseline against the corpus as a background), judge each reading by displacement from the enrollment rather than by content, calibrate in d′. The nurse = the enrolled speaker; the corpus = the background; reader-delta = the verification score. Honest arithmetic: the fleet's enrollment is a **median**, not an adapted Gaussian mixture — a strictly weaker but cleaner estimator — so what is borrowed is GMM-UBM's logic, with a simpler statistic standing in for its model.
- **What is genuinely new — said plainly.** By itself, the reader-delta object *is* GMM-UBM with a twist, and the twist is Rival A's finding: operationally it reduces to per-reader normalization of outputs, which is the enrollment step of GMM-UBM before the mixture is fitted. Two things survive as residual: **(i) the question the displacement answers.** In GMM-UBM, displacement answers *"is this utterance from the enrolled speaker?"* — evidence about the individual herself. Here, displacement answers *"what happened in the room, as filtered through a known model?"* — evidence about a third object, which is the Nurse doctrine's second JEPA and is not a question speaker verification poses. **(ii) The retrieval-key placement** (the doctor reads the nurse's delta as his orientation for entering the room; the delta indexes memory rather than gating a claim). Both residuals are architectural/placement claims, not instrument claims, and both are currently fixture-conditional pending the Antecedent Test and the Switch Test.

### A4. MLOps drift monitoring (PSI, KS tests, stream drift detectors)

- **What it is.** The production-ML product category: monitoring whether a deployed model's inputs or outputs have displaced from a calibration baseline, via population-stability indices (credit-scoring practice), two-sample Kolmogorov–Smirnov tests (Kolmogorov 1933; Smirnov 1948), and streaming drift detectors — J. Gama, P. Medas, G. Castillo, P. Rodrigues, "Learning with Drift Detection," SBIA 2004 (LNCS — volume flagged below), the DDM detector and its descendants.
- **What the dissertation borrows.** "Has this known model displaced from its calibration baseline" as an existing, dashboard-grade capability; and, per Rival A's pointed observation, **per-agent baselining as standard practice** — "every production drift detector in existence does per-agent baselining." Any claim that baseline-relative reading is novel perishes here first.
- **What is genuinely new.** The placement only (same as A1–A3): the monitored object is a reader's *reading* rather than a model's input/output distribution, and the drift signal is repurposed as a retrieval key. Nothing in the detector mathematics is claimed.

## B. The first-order retrieval and training family

### B1. FaceNet — triplet-loss metric learning

- **What it is.** The computer-vision embedding that made metric learning mainstream: an end-to-end learned embedding in which anchor–positive distance is trained to be smaller than anchor–negative distance (triplet loss), so that identity becomes geometry. F. Schroff, D. Kalenichenko, J. Philbin, "FaceNet: A Unified Embedding for Face Recognition and Clustering," CVPR 2015.
- **What the dissertation borrows.** The contrast head's entire frame (already named in Chapter 5): anchor/near/far training structure, embedding-space discrimination, and the evaluation discipline. Also — less flatteringly — the **known failure mode**: the devil's "FaceNet suspicion" (that the head's in-sample margin was identity memorization, not generalization) was *confirmed* in the held-out re-test (Ch5 §5.5: in-sample 0.478 collapsing to 0.03–0.11 on held-out nights, one seed below the noise floor). The dissertation's encoder tier is currently a FaceNet-mode failure, and the related-work entry must say so, not just cite the success half.
- **What is genuinely new.** The embedded object — a room-temperature field (vMF snapshot / dial-bank state, per C2 below) rather than a face or a sentence — and the discriminative target (cross-room sauna/plunge gap rather than person identity). This is an application claim, honestly sized; the loss, the architecture, and the failure mode are FaceNet's.

### B2. InfoNCE / contrastive learning

- **What it is.** The dominant self-supervised objective: learn representations by identifying the true sample among negatives (InfoNCE), from A. van den Oord, Y. Li, O. Vinyals, "Representation Learning with Contrastive Predictive Coding," arXiv:1807.03748, 2018; ancestor: noise-contrastive estimation, M. Gutmann & A. Hyvärinen, AISTATS 2010.
- **What the dissertation borrows.** Law 2 (**contrast only**) is InfoNCE at room grain: same-room positives, different-room negatives, validation on cross-room gaps. The doctrine's claim that the learnable quantity exists *only between* rooms (the elephant is invisible from inside) is precisely the contrastive premise; the deadman switch is that premise pre-registered as an executable protocol.
- **What is genuinely new.** Not the loss. The candidates: (i) the pairing unit — the room/field as the positive-defining container, rather than token/context or image/augmentation; (ii) the *pre-registered refusal* to treat within-room ordering as an objective (retirement of the 0.849 metric), which is a doctrinal choice, not a training innovation; (iii) the honest tier inversion (dial-tier vs encoder-tier geometry are different geometries). Adjacent, not committee-named: BYOL (J.-B. Grill et al., NeurIPS 2020) and the JEPA family (B3) achieve embedding learning *without* contrast — worth one sentence in the dissertation because the doctrine's namesake is contrast-free while its training law is contrast-only; see the flagged list.

### B3. JEPA — Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture

- **What it is.** Y. LeCun's proposal to learn world models by predicting in representation space (predict the embedding of masked/future content, never reconstruct pixels or words), avoiding contrastive collapse via regularized predictors rather than negatives: Y. LeCun, "A Path Towards Autonomous Machine Intelligence" (OpenReview position paper, 2022 — exact version flagged below); instantiated as I-JEPA (M. Assran et al., CVPR 2023) and V-JEPA (video, 2024).
- **What the dissertation borrows.** The name, and the doctrine: the Nurse memo's two readings are JEPA *structurally* — comparable features across instances, prediction of the shape in representation space, "words are constraints; JEPA is a likeness." The dissertation's theory of what a reading *is* (first reading: nurse→patient likeness; second reading: doctor→nurse drift) is borrowed wholesale from this frame.
- **What is genuinely new — and the honest tension.** **No fleet component implements a JEPA architecture.** The machinery trained to date is contrastive (B2/B1 family); "JEPA" names the dissertation's *object of study* — a reading as a prediction in representation space — not any architecture shipped. The dissertation must state this plainly: the namesake is doctrinal, the training is InfoNCE. What is genuinely new is the **second-JEPA claim itself** — that reading a *known reader's* drift is a distinct epistemic object from reading the room (a JEPA of a JEPA) — and its empirical status is: real on fixtures, conditional on an unmeasured premise, margin over normalized first-order definitional (§A3). The claim is the dissertation's most original contribution *and* its most provisional result; both halves must travel together.

## C. The calibration and estimation family

### C1. d′ / signal detection theory, with Mahalanobis D″

- **What it is.** The psychophysics framework separating sensitivity from criterion: d′ = z(hit rate) − z(false-alarm rate). D. M. Green & J. A. Swets, *Signal Detection Theory and Psychophysics* (Wiley, 1966); application to human detection: W. P. Tanner & J. A. Swets, "A decision-making theory of visual detection," *Psychological Review* 61, 1954; modern reference: N. A. Macmillan & C. D. Creelman, *Detection Theory: A User's Guide*, 2nd ed., 2005. The multivariate calibration distance is P. C. Mahalanobis, "On the Generalised Distance in Statistics," *Proceedings of the National Institute of Sciences of India* 2:49–55, 1936.
- **What the dissertation borrows.** All of it, as pure machinery: the D″ calibration (aggregate Mahalanobis d′ = 17.99, per-pair 13.2–26.8), the D′ cold-entry acclimation curve (half-life ≈ 20 msgs), permutation floors standing in for ROC sweeps, and speaker-verification-style calibrated reporting (d′/EER conventions per A3; DET-curve provenance flagged below).
- **What is genuinely new.** **Nothing.** This ancestor is borrowed machinery end to end — the honest entry says so without a residual clause. The only new thing in its neighborhood is *what* gets calibrated (reader-delta discrimination), which is A1–A3's residual, not this entry's.

### C2. von Mises–Fisher distribution and directional estimation

- **What it is.** The vMF distribution on the hypersphere — mean direction μ, concentration κ — the canonical model for directional data. Circular case: R. von Mises, 1918 (exact citation flagged); sphere case: R. A. Fisher, "Dispersion on a Sphere," *Proceedings of the Royal Society A* 217, 1953; modern canonical text: K. V. Mardia & P. E. Jupp, *Directional Statistics* (Wiley, 2000). The maximum-likelihood κ approximation used as the Newton initializer in `vmf.py` is from A. Banerjee, I. S. Dhillon, J. Ghosh, S. Sra, "Clustering on the Unit Hypersphere using von Mises-Fisher Distributions," *JMLR* 6:1345–1382, 2005 — which is also prior art for "summarize embeddings as vMF."
- **What the dissertation borrows.** The whole estimator of Chapter 2: (μ̂, κ) with A_d(κ)=ρ solved by Newton, Banerjee init, bootstrap CIs, the small-sample honesty guards. The math is textbook (Mardia & Jupp) plus one shipped numerical patch (the sinh-overflow clamp), which is engineering, not theory.
- **What is genuinely new.** Two constructions, honestly sized as modest: (i) the **decoupling-by-construction** — warmth reads μ̂ by projection only, κ reads ρ only, and rotation-invariance of ρ makes the warmth/tightness collinearity (the banned v0 proxy's sickness) impossible by design rather than corrected downstream; (ii) the **jackknife-SE deadband** as an operational edge criterion (an edge is real iff ‖Δμ̂‖ > 2·SE(μ̂); stillness is a reading). The application domain (conversational dial banks as the sample on S⁶) is new but is an application claim.

## D. The epistemology ancestor

### D1. Agrippa's trilemma / the foundationalist regress

- **What it is.** The classical skeptical argument, attributed to Agrippa the Skeptic (reported in Sextus Empiricus, *Outlines of Pyrrhonism* I.164–169 — section numbers flagged): every attempted ultimate justification ends in infinite regress, circularity, or unproved assumption. The modern form is Hans Albert's "Münchhausen trilemma," *Traktat über kritische Vernunft*, 1968 (Eng.: *Treatise on Critical Reason*); adjacent: K. R. Popper's critical rationalism, *The Logic of Scientific Discovery* (1959 English edition), for the falsification discipline this dissertation's registered tests descend from.
- **What the dissertation borrows.** The problem's shape, wholesale: "who reads the doctor?" is Agrippa's regress in a committee's clothing. Any interpreter-chain terminates in regress, circle, or dogma — the devil's pass-3 demand (blind reader-delta test or the doctrine moves to the acknowledgments) is the trilemma converted into a pre-registration.
- **What is genuinely new.** The **operationalization**: the dissertation treats the regress not as a philosophical datum but as an *empirical convergence question* — the corrective map has a fixed point iff corrections shrink (measured: they are not; corrections/day rose 73%, the 74% rate decline is a denominator artifact — "flooding, not settling"); the chain terminates in non-interpretive layers (the room-instrument that measures, the archive that records) plus convergence-or-convention, with the rebound falsifier registered (next-30d correction rate > 0.15 ⇒ the "fixed point" was an artifact). Turning the trilemma into a falsifiable measurement protocol with booked negative results is a real residual — possibly the sturdiest one in this file, precisely because its current status is *specified, registered, and unmet* rather than claimed-won.

## Positioning

**Where this dissertation sits.** The first-order story is assembled entirely from existing parts: rooms are summarized with textbook vMF directional statistics (C2), retrieval is FaceNet-style metric learning trained with an InfoNCE objective at room grain (B1–B2), and every sensitivity claim is calibrated with fifty-year-old signal-detection machinery (C1) — none of which is claimed as new, and one of which (the encoder tier's held-out FAIL) currently exhibits exactly its ancestor's known failure mode. The second-order story — the Nurse doctrine's reader-delta, the dissertation's claimed addition — is, by the committee's own arithmetic, an old instrument pointed at a new object: the displacement-from-baseline idea is Shewhart's and CUSUM's, the individual-baseline-plus-error-judgment is Jacobson–Truax's, the enroll-and-displace structure is GMM-UBM's, and the practice is industrial-standard in MLOps drift monitoring (A1–A4); operationally it reduces to per-reader normalization, which is not novel in itself. What survives as the dissertation's own, stated at the bar both reviewers set: **(1)** the *placement* — the monitored process is another reader's readings of a third object, and the displacement is consumed as a **retrieval key** (the doctor's orientation; the nurse as the index), a question none of the drift ancestors pose; **(2)** the *operationalized regress* (D1) — a convergence-or-convention termination condition with registered falsifiers and booked negative numbers; **(3)** the *conditional fixture result* — given idiosyncratic baselines (unmeasured in the field; kill band 0.3–0.6 corpus-sd), baseline-relative reading unlocks what raw output similarity demonstrably cannot do (Rival A's own run: r = 0.456 ≈ chance). Every one of the three carries its condition welded on.

**One sentence:** *first-order retrieval is FaceNet/InfoNCE over vMF snapshots — all borrowed; the claimed addition is not a new instrument but a new placement of a century-old one (Shewhart/CUSUM/RCI/GMM-UBM drift-from-baseline) — pointing it at a known reader's readings and using her displacement as a retrieval key — proven only on fixtures that instantiate its own premise, with the field's baseline-spread-to-drift ratio (0.3–0.6 corpus-sd kill band) as the single number that decides whether the claim survives.*
